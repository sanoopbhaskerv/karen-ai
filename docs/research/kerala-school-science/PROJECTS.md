## How to interpret the evidence

An NCERT catalogue entry establishes that an exhibit was included in that national exhibition's published list. It does not establish a Kerala Sasthrolsavam prize, a particular qualifying route, independently verified performance, or which pupil wrote each part of the code. School location is recorded separately from the route: Kerala-located JNV, KV and other school-system exhibits must not automatically be described as Kerala state-fair selections. Preserve the source's pupil and teacher credits together; the division of their work remains unknown. The inspected 2010–2017 catalogue rows name schools but omit pupil and teacher credits; empty credit arrays for the seven selected cases from these books mean the names were not supplied, not that no pupils or teachers participated.

These are short factual paraphrases with links to the actual pages, not copies of complete abstracts. The catalogues supply concise descriptions rather than reproducible engineering reports. In the tables, the final column identifies evidence **not supplied on the cited page**; it does not assert that the pupils never collected it.

## What Karen can learn from these cases

The following are **our recommendations**, not judging rules or claims made by the catalogue authors:

- Explain the complete chain: problem → input or observation → decision/computation → output or action → test. A sensor, controller, machine-learning model and cloud service have different roles.
- Distinguish a demonstrated operation from a proposed application. A gesture-controlled game does not establish safe wheelchair control; a notification prototype does not establish connection to an emergency dispatch service.
- Ask for a measurement and comparison before claiming “more accurate”, “safer”, “cheaper” or “saves energy”. Record conditions, sample size, failures and uncertainty.
- Attribute reused hardware, software, libraries, models and teacher help. A catalogue's pupil credit is not a construction log or source-code authorship record.
- Keep the event, year, category and award scope attached to the fact. A national catalogue entry and a Kerala state-fair placing answer different questions.

For Karen's own working-model presentation, these cases support explaining hardware and software contribution clearly. They do not grant eligibility or establish the current competition's team rules; use [RULES.md](RULES.md) and [KAREN_ENTRY.md](KAREN_ENTRY.md) for those questions.

## Coverage and next collection priorities

Full *List of Exhibits* catalogue PDFs for 2010–2019, 2022, 2023 and 2024 were downloaded and text-extracted. Relevant complete pages were inspected, with representative visual checks for each year except 2022 in the initial pass; the follow-up also checked two 2022 pages visually. Nineteen method-bearing catalogue entries are now indexed; this is a selected sample rather than an enumeration of every Kerala entry. Kerala school matches were found in every reviewed 2010–2019 catalogue, even where no case was selected for this index. The English school-address columns of the older books were readable; problematic legacy-font Hindi extraction was not needed for these attributions. All 55 chapter author/school blocks in the separate 2015–2017 *Structure and Working* books were reviewed; no Kerala-attributed chapter was identified among those selected reports. Their absence from that search does not contradict the Kerala entries found in the different *List of Exhibits* publications. Kerala hosting a book's exhibition is not a Kerala attribution for its exhibits. All sources are linked in the source register.
