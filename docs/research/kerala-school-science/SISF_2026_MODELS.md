# SISF 2026: Kerala published model write-ups

Checked on **1 November 2026**. SCERT Telangana’s [official compendium](https://www.scert.telangana.gov.in/Pdf/publication/SouthernIndiasciencefair2026-ExhibitsCompendium.pdf) (`pathways-37`) contains **31 Kerala write-ups: 14 individual, 7 group and 10 teacher exhibits**, spanning 49 content pages. The [structured index](sisf-2026-models.json) records every title, school, published credit, complete page range, reported method and evidence limitation in those sections. These counts describe the received book, not a quota or a claim that every selected entrant submitted a write-up. Teacher exhibits remain separate from pupil work.

All 49 content pages were read in extracted text; all 31 attribution blocks and 26 full method/result pages were checked against rendered source images. Actual headings and page footers establish the categories: individual PDF 193, group PDF 219 and teacher PDF 233. The first teacher write-up is printed 192 / PDF 235; the contents table’s printed 204 is misleading. Contacts, photographs and page images are excluded from the repository.

## Keep claims and capabilities separate

These are reported designs and studies, with uneven detail. The rubber study supplies numerical comparisons; most electronics reports omit repeat trials, error rates and raw measurements. Classroom comparisons also omit information needed to establish a causal learning benefit. The per-entry limits in the index distinguish missing evidence from a claim that the authors never gathered it.

The medication dispenser describes ESP32, C++, Firebase schedules, NTP and motor control; its title alone does not establish AI. Pickup sensing and camera authentication are proposed improvements. Hourly schedule fetching also needs reconciling with the report’s immediate-update claim. The driving simulator uses ETS2 and Arduino Leonardo inputs; AI traffic and custom software are listed as future work. SAMARTH likewise proposes offline edge AI as an improvement. HOPE’s LoRa link reaches a base station, while its cloud notifications still use Wi-Fi/MQTT.

Fog-water potability, diaper skin safety, safe wheelchair use and real-road training benefit are not established by these write-ups. The water-indicator study does not identify a pathogenic amoeba or validate outbreak prediction. The microplastics chapter’s morphology percentages have unclear denominators; they must not be combined as exclusive shares.
