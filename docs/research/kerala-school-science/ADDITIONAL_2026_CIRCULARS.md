# Additional 2026 departmental circulars

## Subdistrict Mathematics Quiz and Talent Search: date amendment

The DGE’s [one-page circular of 8 September 2026](https://education.kerala.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sciencequiz892026.pdf), **DGE/8659/2026-Y1; I/1905146/2026**, moves the **subdistrict Mathematics Quiz and Talent Search examinations** from **19 September to Wednesday, 23 September 2026**, citing technical difficulties with the original date. It directs the competitions to follow the 2026–27 action plan with this amendment. The entire page was extracted, rendered and read visually.

| Field | Precisely supported scope |
| --- | --- |
| Stream and events | Mathematics Quiz and Talent Search examinations |
| Level | Subdistrict |
| Original planned date | 19 September 2026 |
| Amended planned date | 23 September 2026, printed as Wednesday |
| Referenced correspondence | Mathematics Club state secretary’s letter of 3 September 2026 |
| Circulation | DDEs; AEOs through DDEs; Mathematics Club secretaries; KITE for official publication |

The filename contains `sciencequiz`, but the Malayalam subject and body explicitly name **Mathematics**. This amendment must not be transferred to Science Quiz, Science Working Model or the general fair windows. It qualifies the original Mathematics dates in the [mirrored action plan](CALENDAR_2026.md); it does not replace the Science calendar.

The official website post is dated **10 September 2026**, while the circular and its visible digital-signature block are dated **8 September**. The block reads **Santhosh C A, 10:57:48**, with the document issued for the Director of General Education. The signature block was inspected visually; cryptographic signature validation was not performed.

The circular supplies no item codes, class-section allocation, examination duration, reporting time, venue or changed pupil count. It establishes a scheduled date amendment, not proof that examinations were held or that particular pupils attended. It is not a SISF or NCERT nomination/delegation order; those recent Kerala proceedings remain unestablished by this document.


## IT Quiz: original schedule and later subdistrict amendment

KITE’s [31 July 2026 circular](https://kite.kerala.gov.in/KITE/itsadmin/uploads/docs/1132.pdf), **KITE/2026/1275(6)**, gives the school, subdistrict and revenue-district IT Quiz schedule. A separate [7 September circular, public mirror](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1n8zFdAwBahQtafXjVtNUIA0qsyzTISmU/view), **KITE/2026/1275(9)**, moves only the subdistrict competition from **9 to 10 September**, explicitly keeping the time schedule unchanged. The mirror’s visible official content is retained without claiming an independently authenticated signature or recovered official-hosted copy.

The [six-row versioned schedule](it-quiz-schedule-2026.json) retains the original and amended dates. These are scheduled starts, not durations, actual attendance, Karen’s check-in instructions or proof the events occurred. Higher Secondary is abbreviated HSS in the index; the source does not explicitly print VHSS in that row. The absence of a district UP row is not, alone, a complete eligibility rule. Later amendments are not exhaustively ruled out by these two documents.

The July circular directs school questions to be prepared in **SRG under the headteachers’ leadership**. Subdistrict and district use **KITE-prepared common questions in presentation format**. It does not supply a portal URL/login or require portal-based conduct. The September amendment gives no reason for postponement or new participant limit. Both pages were read visually and independently checked; their handwritten signatures are labelled Chief Executive Officer, without a legible personal name being inferred.

This is an **IT Quiz** amendment. It does not change Mathematics Quiz, Science Quiz, prepared Working Model or the general fair windows. The separate Mathematics amendment above moves its named subdistrict examinations from 19 to 23 September. Keep the two dates and streams separate.
