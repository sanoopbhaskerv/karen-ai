## Other streams and source anomalies

Pages 3–5 cover Social Science dates and topics. Its general fair windows are **1–7 September at school**, **22–30 September at subdistrict** and **26–31 October at district**. News reading and talent-search phases extend into November/December. These dates must not be substituted for Science dates. Listed LP album/chart/collection topics do not by themselves resolve the separate manual-versus-registration-catalogue discrepancy in [RULE_CHANGES.md](RULE_CHANGES.md#lp-mappings-that-require-care).

Pages 6–7 cover Mathematics, including a subdistrict fair target **by 12 October**, district target **by 28 October**, and the **18–21 November** state fair. State talent search, seminars and paper presentations are separately planned for **5 or 6 December**. The source prints an UP quiz slot as **11.30–12.30 am**; this is retained as a source anomaly rather than silently changed to pm. The printed weekday labels for 19 September and 1 October agree with the 2026 calendar. A later [8 September DGE circular](ADDITIONAL_2026_CIRCULARS.md) moves subdistrict Mathematics Quiz and Talent Search from 19 to **23 September**; the original action-plan date is retained as a superseded plan, not current instruction. Science page 2 labels its 28 February 2027 line as “Natural Science Day” in Malayalam; this index preserves that wording rather than silently changing it to National Science Day.

Page 8 covers Work Experience, including subdistrict fairs in the **first week of October** and later exhibition/club activities. Page 9 has only the reference/footer. No IT calendar section occurs in this attachment. The different stream calendars and December events prevent an unqualified claim that every activity ends on 21 November.
