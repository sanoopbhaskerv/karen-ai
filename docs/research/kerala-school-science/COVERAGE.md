## Highest priority documents from the school or organisers

1. **A redacted school entry confirmation.** Retain fair, exact item name/code, competition section, participant count and organising authority. Keep pupil names, school identity, class section, home details, admission numbers and credentials out of the public research copy and assistant context.
2. **The 2026 subdistrict notice and Working Model marking sheet.** Include date, venue, reporting time, display requirements, any amendments, electricity/network arrangements, construction-video instructions and whether both registered pupils must attend throughout.
3. **Any written clarification on connected exhibits.** For Karen, useful subjects are use of a phone hotspot, a laptop/server, external speech/AI services, dependency disclosure and a fallback demonstration. The inspected general Working Model provisions do not settle all of these details.
4. **Historical souvenirs and final result books.** Scans of title pages, publication details, annual chronology and consolidated championship pages would help resolve origin and result conflicts. The recovered 2012 souvenir-advertising request is not the completed souvenir.
5. **Student project reports and logs.** A report with the problem, experiment/design, student contribution, measured results and sources adds more useful knowledge than an award photograph alone. The manual’s state souvenir chapter directs publication of Sasthrolsava Rekha with exhibit notes/photos and office/directorate copies; published souvenirs are a concrete archival lead, without implying every edition survives.

The intended entry is Science Fair Working Model following the user's latest correction. The earlier IT description remains useful only as a reminder to verify the registered name. Hardware does not, by itself, prove classification; the dated rules and accepted school entry do.

## Source access limits already investigated

The 2026 programme-notice and Vocational Expo destinations linked from the state portal returned HTTP 404. Several official PDFs timed out in browser tooling but were successfully downloaded directly and rendered; those were reviewed rather than labelled unavailable. The special-school manual is scanned and undated in the inspected material. Historical teacher/association archives and contemporary newspapers are labelled as such instead of being presented as signed government records.

No complete public corpus of every Kerala exhibition project report has been established. The result portals expose participation and scores but generally do not supply the full scientific work. The ten recovered NCERT edited write-ups and 31 Kerala SISF 2026 descriptions add construction/method evidence but not original school logs or independent test results. Their counts overlap the focused case index; teacher entries remain distinct from pupil projects. Further collection should investigate school archives, souvenirs and official demonstrations while preserving attribution and publication context.
