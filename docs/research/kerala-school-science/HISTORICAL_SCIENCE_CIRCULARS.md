## C. V. Raman essay conditions and topics

PDF 3–4 provides a three-hour reference phase using internet/books, followed by one hour of writing and two minutes of interaction. The essay directions prohibit ChatGPT/AI-assisted websites during that process. Essay and interaction marks determine the result. The state topic is supplied at competition time; the earlier-stage lists below do not predetermine it.

| Section | School topics, PDF 4–5 | Subdistrict topics, PDF 4–5 | Revenue-district topics, PDF 4–5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HS | Sustainable development/basic science; ocean pollution; green energy/environment protection | Antibiotic resistance; plastic/microplastic pollution; climate and biodiversity | Raman’s legacy; sustainable agriculture/biotechnology; space research |
| HSS/VHSE | Zero-waste living; renewable energy; waste policy/management | Stem-cell therapy; periodic table; dark matter/energy | 5G; polymers; AI/big data in disaster forecasting |

The English line on PDF 4 prints **“Green energy and environment conversion”**; its Malayalam wording concerns conservation. The summary above retains the meaning without silently changing the printed English title.

The AI restriction is attached to this historical essay event. It does not establish a 2026 prepared Working Model rule. Use [the current rules guide](RULES.md) and an applicable current organiser notice for that entry.
