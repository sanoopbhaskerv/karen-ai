# Earlier IT competitions: instructions and archived publication evidence

Reviewed **1 November 2026**. Two recovered DPI circulars add primary evidence before the [2009 integration manual](HISTORY.md#the-2009-integration-milestone). They concern historical **IT competitions**, rather than a full annual programme or results for every science-fair stream. Both six-page files were preserved from the Education Department's former official website; all twelve pages were read visually.

The follow-up below adds archived official event-site pages and six item-result tables. Their HTML bodies, links and table structure were read directly; no browser-render review or validation of the referenced photographs is claimed. A further six-page government retrospective supplies the earlier IT-stream statements below.

## Government retrospective: IT fairs in 2002–04

The Education Department's archived [IT@School activities till date](https://web.archive.org/web/20080719181157id_/http://www.education.kerala.gov.in/master/progressitschool.pdf), source `history-2000s-012`, carries year headings rather than a printed issue date. Under **2002**, PDF 1(vii), it calls the fair at **Thiruvalla** the state's first IT fair, held as part of the State School Science Fair. Under **2003**, PDF/printed 2(x), it reports the science fair and second IT fair successfully held at **Ernakulam**. Under **2004**, PDF/printed 3(ix), it records an associated IT fair at **Kottayam**, without an ordinal.

All six pages were visually read. The received **19 July 2008** Memento date matches its cached CDX row and body digest. PDF metadata dated January 2006 and references to future July 2006 activity are not adopted as an issue date. These are a government retrospective's IT-stream claims, rather than original event circulars, exact event days, a confirmed academic-year sequence or Science Fair inaugural evidence. The missing earlier circulars and souvenir remain unrecovered.

## IT competitions alongside science fairs, 2004–05

[DPI circular NEP (3)-71186/04/DPI, 18 September 2004](https://web.archive.org/web/20050314041551id_/http://www.education.kerala.gov.in:80/newitems/circular-%20state%20level%20science%20fair%202004.pdf), PDF 1, directs computer-education competitions for 2004–05 alongside district/state science fairs. It describes three student on-the-spot items, IT quiz and a teacher multimedia item. PDF 1 restricts the student on-the-spot section to classes 8–10. The circular's reference to activity during the preceding three years is an administrative retrospective, **not an identified inaugural date**.

PDF 2 explicitly leaves the **Kerala State Science Fair's date and venue to a later announcement**. PDF 4 only tentatively schedules district competitions during **November 2004**. Neither supplies the state venue, exact state dates, results or proof that every planned contest occurred.

PDF 5 is a blank **Kerala State IT Fair 2004–2005** entry form; PDF 6 contains historical evaluation criteria. PDF 4 assigns equipment coordination to district science-club secretaries and IT@School trainers and carries a handwritten block labelled “for Director of Public Instruction”; no personal signatory name is inferred. This page is image-only and would be missed by relying on the file's extracted text. Source `history-2000s-001`.

## State School IT Fest scheduled for December 2007

[DPI circular NEP(3)/82862/07DPI, 5 December 2007](https://web.archive.org/web/20080828012615id_/http://www.education.kerala.gov.in/itschool/circular_itfair.pdf), PDF 1 and the blank entry form on PDF 6, schedule **State School IT Fest 2007 at Techno Park, Thiruvananthapuram, 29–31 December 2007**. The document concerns students and teachers of high schools during **2007–08**; the archive capture's August 2008 date is not the event date.

PDF 1–3 describe programming, student multimedia, digital painting, IT projects, IT quiz and teacher multimedia. PDF 2 calls for project reports/slides by **24 December 2007**, with a ten-minute presentation and five-minute discussion. PDF 5 includes student involvement and project genuineness in its historical project-evaluation scheme.

PDF 4 separately provides for selected pupil inventions/software/hardware to be demonstrated and assigns arrangements to IT@School. That provision does not establish a present event named “IT Working Model” or classify Karen's Arduino project. Its handwritten signature is above the DPI designation; no personal name or cryptographic validation is established. These are scheduled arrangements, **not attendance, results or proof of completed events**. Source `history-2000s-002`.

Its linked [second photo page](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109221704id_/http://www.education.kerala.gov.in:80/itfest/photos_2.htm) labels **“DAY 2 : 31st December 2007”**. These are captions in received HTML bodies, preserved on **9 January 2008**. The referenced photographs and banner were not independently reviewed. Sources `history-2000s-003` and `history-2000s-004`.

Keep these captions alongside the circular's announced **29–31 December 2007** range. They do not establish that the opening was moved, explain the differing Day 1 label, prove every scheduled contest happened, or turn the January capture date into the event date.

The [archived result index](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109081153id_/http://www.education.kerala.gov.in:80/itfest/result.htm) links the six tables below and advertises a brochure and a programme file with `26.12.2007` in its filename. Those two advertised bodies were not recovered; a filename is not a verified printed issue date or a recovered programme. Source `history-2000s-005`.

## Provenance and limits

The [source register](history-2000s-sources.json) preserves issue dates separately from archive captures, PDF sizes/page counts, SHA-256 hashes, original URLs and page locators. Cached acquisition headers give actual Memento dates matching the requested captures, and each received body's SHA-1/base32 digest matches its CDX entry. This verifies the archived-body correspondence; it does not authenticate a handwritten signature or establish later amendments.

The same actual-header, SHA-256 and CDX SHA-1/base32 checks were applied to the nine newly adopted HTML bodies. One page named `downloads.htm` contains blank result templates, rather than the promised older notices, and is not treated as a populated result list. The advertised banner, brochure and programme bodies remain unreviewed/unrecovered.

The 2004 file has four instruction pages, a blank form and an evaluation attachment with its own printed pagination. The numbering jumps from teacher provision 7 to 9 across PDF 3–4; no missing provision is reconstructed. A third archived `itfair.pdf` was received as an unreadable/truncated PDF and is **not registered or used as substantive evidence**; it was not requested again in this pass. A different candidate, `it circular 2008.pdf`, also failed PDF parsing despite matching its CDX body digest and remains excluded. A 2008–09 IT-result mirror mentioning January 2009 remains outside the adopted records.

The exact earlier bodies **NEP(3)-68121/03/DPI of 25 September 2003**, **Y1/59986/06/DPI of 9 October 2006** and **Y1/69707/07/DPI of 16 October 2007** remain unrecovered. Their dates are references printed in later circulars, not independently verified issue dates from those missing bodies. The bounded follow-up screened archive URL metadata and advertised links; absence from those results does not establish that an order was never issued or published.

These historical software, eligibility, timing and evaluation conditions do not replace the current Science Working Model manual or local registration instructions. No complete annual chronology, state science-fair championship or inaugural statewide-fair claim follows from these circulars.
