## Judges and evaluation records

The general judging section requires **three judges at every level**, impartial and suitably qualified, with a biodata/declaration in the prescribed format. It restricts judging at successive levels: a subdistrict judge cannot judge the same revenue district's fair, and a district judge cannot judge the state fair in the same year. DGE oversees state/district selection; DDEs appoint subdistrict judges using the stated criteria. The paragraph also states a two-year appointment limit; this summary does not add a consecutive-year reset that the inspected wording does not state. [Printed 6–7 / PDF 17–18](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=17)

Subdistrict score corrections must be signed by the evaluator. Evaluation records with comments accompany score sheets; mark lists and tabulation sheets are sealed and retained at the **AEO office for one year**, available when required by the appeal committee or higher authorities. District evaluation records are retained for one year at the **DDE office**. These retention provisions do not establish unrestricted public access to pupils' personal records. [Printed 13–14 / PDF 24–25](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=24)

The programme committee collects score, remarks and tabulation sheets and stage-manager reports, prepares results, and arranges uploads. It provides contest rules before an event and can disqualify a participant who fails to appear at the required place/time. Organiser contest recordings are separate from a pupil's required construction video. [Printed 20–22 / PDF 31–33](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=31)

For deadlines, fees and refund conditions for an actual appeal, use [RULES.md](RULES.md#grading-championship-and-appeals) and the applicable local procedure.

## Food, accommodation, transport and welfare

The state committee chapter assigns food provision to the food committee, including food at stalls on evaluation day and food packets for on-the-spot contestants. The accommodation committee allocates registered teams' lodging and arranges water, light, washing and toilet facilities. Registration coordinates cards, food coupons and accommodation information. Welfare arrangements cover first aid/medical assistance, cleanliness and drinking water; transport arrangements connect venues and arriving/departing groups. [Printed 23 and 26–27 / PDF 34 and 37–38](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=34)

These are state organisational responsibilities, not evidence that a specific subdistrict offers overnight lodging, a particular meal schedule, free individual transport or cash travel reimbursement.

General safety rules apply from school through state. The manual requires green-protocol conduct and permits disciplinary action for disruptive behaviour. Venue construction/fitness and electrical certificates belong to organiser arrangements. [Printed 5 and 7 / PDF 16 and 18](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=16); [printed 24 and 28 / PDF 35 and 39](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=35)

## Certificates and an archival lead

For a lost or corrected **DGE-issued** certificate, the manual prescribes an application through the headteacher/principal with a ₹250 demand draft in the DGE's name. This is distinct from entry or appeal fees. [Printed 6 / PDF 17](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=17)

The souvenir committee must prepare the *Sasthrolsava Rekha* using articles, photographs and competitors' exhibit notes, submit the publication within a month after the event, and provide copies to education offices and the higher-secondary/vocational directorates. [Printed 29 / PDF 40](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=40)

That is a concrete lead for locating older project descriptions and programmes. It does not prove that every required edition was published, survives, or is publicly downloadable. Seek published copies or catalogue information rather than pupils' private registration files.
