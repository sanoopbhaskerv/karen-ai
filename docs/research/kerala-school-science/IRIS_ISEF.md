## What the IRIS pages specify

The guidelines/FAQ describe Indian-origin pupils residing and studying at India-based schools in **classes 5–12**, entering individually or as exactly two students. They require one guide and original research with experimental data, or a novel engineering improvement. Display-only models without research and surveys without personal experimentation are excluded. Experimental data are limited to the preceding 12 months; a pupil previously registered as Class 12 cannot reapply. These are IRIS statements, not Kerala General Education eligibility rules. [Guidelines](https://iris.exstemplar.com/guidelines.html), [FAQ](https://iris.exstemplar.com/faqs.html)

The public submission overview asks for a synopsis PDF, full research-paper PDF and **YouTube URL for a video of at most 90 seconds**, public or unlisted. Guidelines/FAQ state PDF limits of 5 MB and 20 MB and suggest roughly 10–15 paper pages without a fixed word limit. The synopsis guidance is:

The registration overview also describes a 300-word abstract cap. No logged-in enforcement, upload, payment or submission was tested. Its pre-study-synopsis teaching explanation does not turn an untested proposal into completed experimental evidence. IRIS's 90-second presentation video is separate from Kerala Working Model's maximum-five-minute construction-participation video. [Guidelines](https://iris.exstemplar.com/guidelines.html), [registration overview](https://register.irisnationalfair.org/)

The FAQ advertises **₹5,000 plus applicable taxes per project**, an EWS waiver with documentation and limited refund wording. These are dated publisher statements, not a verified payable quote, granted waiver or guaranteed travel support. The closed-window observation does not prove a later reopening. ## Programme-specific anonymity

IRIS's guidelines/FAQ forbid school, city and state names in the project video, paper/report, presentation and abstract. Booth guidance also prohibits student identity disclosure; the FAQ distinguishes judging from public sessions where names/schools may be shared. ## ISEF 2026–2027 rules inspected

The linked Society for Science book has 46 physical pages. ISEF requires affiliated-fair selection, grades **9–12 or equivalent**, and not reaching 20 on or before the preceding May 1. Its maximum-three team rule does not override IRIS's stricter two-person entry. IRIS class eligibility alone does not establish ISEF eligibility. [Printed 3 / PDF 4](https://sspcdn.blob.core.windows.net/files/Documents/SEP/ISEF/2027/Rules/Book.pdf#page=4)

The book permits acknowledged AI resources, requires independent work in pupils' own words, and bars generative AI from writing the research plan, abstract, poster or citations. **Student Support Disclosure Form 2A**, required for all projects, covers adult/paid support and generative AI. Generative-AI use requires an attachment and prompt/response log with personal identifying material removed; it requests a summary if the log is inaccessible. [Printed 3 and 34 / PDF 4 and 35](https://sspcdn.blob.core.windows.net/files/Documents/SEP/ISEF/2027/Rules/Book.pdf#page=35)

ISEF's exhibit must remain visible during operating hours without electricity or internet. This display requirement does not say an entire cloud AI system must operate offline. Affiliated fairs may add conditions. These provisions do not replace subdistrict rules; this targeted review is not a complete protocol/display-safety audit.
