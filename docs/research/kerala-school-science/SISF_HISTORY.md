# Southern India Science Fair in VITM annual reports

Checked on **1 November 2026**. This supplement adds six official VITM reports and reuses the already registered 2022–23 report: **seven reporting cycles, 684 physical PDF pages**. Targeted review covers **26 visual pages**. The [structured evidence register](sisf-annual-report-evidence.json) distinguishes five specifically reported SISF events from two institutional fair/camp non-conduct statements. It is not a seven-edition attendance list.

## Pandemic reporting cycles

The **2020–21** and **2021–22** reports explicitly say in section 13(a), Science Fair/Camp, that fairs/camps could not be held because of Covid-19. Their adjacent sections separately record online Creative Ability Centre activity and district seminars, including online/offline entries. This supports institution-reported interruption in that programme section; it is not a standalone SISF cancellation order, a Kerala state-fair cancellation order or proof that all school science activities stopped. [2020–21, PDF 23 / printed 22](https://www.vismuseum.gov.in/assets/pdf/activity-report/2020-21-Activity-Report-VITM-SU-PDF.pdf#page=23), [2021–22, PDF 31 / printed 30](https://www.vismuseum.gov.in/assets/pdf/activity-report/2021-22-Activity-Report-VITM-PDF-VERSION-RESIZE-Copy-compressed.pdf#page=31).

See the separate [Kerala pandemic evidence](PANDEMIC_ACTIVITIES.md) and [NCERT virtual-edition chronology](PATHWAYS.md#national-exhibitions-during-the-pandemic-years). A different programme's online event is not evidence of an online Sasthrolsavam or a completed SISF edition.

## Website dates and review limits

VITM's [activity-report index](https://www.vismuseum.gov.in/activity-report.php) has seven links with a Publish Date column. For example, it lists the 2018–19 report under **18 March 2024**. That is a received website-column value, not the January 2019 event date or a verified original issue date. Reporting cycles, printed event dates, PDF metadata and access dates remain separate.

The six new PDFs total **568 pages**; the existing 2022–23 PDF adds **116**. All were screened in extracted text for the relevant programme. Text extraction failed on 2020–21 PDF 13; that page rendered correctly and was read visually. It contains other exhibition/facility captions, while the cited PDF 23 paragraph extracts normally. The review does not authenticate photographs or audit every institutional activity or financial table.

These reports do not provide Kerala's outgoing selected-exhibit annexures, pupil-level attendance, prizes or a bridge from a particular Sasthrolsavam item/rank. Those administrative and original-project gaps remain open.
