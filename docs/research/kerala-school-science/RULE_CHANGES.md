# Rule versions, amendments and catalogue reconciliation

Research cut-off: **31 October 2026**. This archive supplements [RULES.md](RULES.md), which describes the current entrant rules. It separates a document's date, the year of the portal hosting it, and the competition level. A historical rule is evidence about that edition, not permission for a current entry.

For Karen's clarified target, **HS Science Working Model, item 115**, the inspected official manuals support a two-pupil entry. The same requirement is present in the older edition. No inspected 2026 document grants a single-pupil exception for this item. An individual digital event, a school's one qualifying exhibit, and the pupil count in a group entry are different quantities.

### A contradiction already present in the older manual

The 2019 general category rule says LP and UP finish at subdistrict ([printed p. 3 / PDF p. 11](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2024/state/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual.pdf#page=11)). Record this as an internal inconsistency in the older edition. It is insufficient evidence for a simple claim that UP progression changed from district to subdistrict in 2025. ## What the recovered amendment copies actually say

### G.O.(Ms)146/2025/GEDN

The [mirrored order](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ssgsUsTmd0IeDxCZSbrhcoo0y6sl1LjO/view), dated **23 August 2025**, presents existing and amended clauses side by side. Reading the existing column as a new rule would preserve the very wording being replaced. Its Science changes include:

- LP and UP quiz participation joins the two-pupil group arrangement, removing the earlier quiz exception. See PDF pp. 2–3; compare the 2024 and 2025 official catalogue quiz capacities below.
- The HS core event list changes from six to seven; the individual project list removes Mechanical and retains Electronics, Robotics and IoT. Science Magazine is removed from that core list and has separate provisions. See PDF pp. 4–6 and the [compiled manual, printed pp. 40–42 / PDF pp. 51–53](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=51).
- The HSS core list changes from nine to seven and likewise retains the three named digital project categories. See PDF pp. 6–8 and the [compiled manual, printed pp. 42–43 / PDF pp. 53–54](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=53).
- LP Social Science changes to Collections/Charts as one combined alternative, Album Making and Quiz: three events. See PDF pp. 29–30 and the [compiled manual, printed p. 93 / PDF p. 104](https://mela.kite.kerala.gov.in/2025/entry_forms/sasthrolsavam_manual_2025.pdf#page=104).

These list counts are not a replacement for the full item map: teacher contests, talent search, magazine, drama and associated programmes have their own clauses. Nor does the inclusion of IoT or Robotics change a registered Science Working Model into an on-the-spot digital project.

## Current Working Model questions that remain open

The inspected set comprises the official 2019 and 2025 manuals, the recovered 2024/2025 amendment copies, the official 2026 science theme/digital-event/drama circulars, and the catalogue snapshots. It contains **no located Working Model amendment** that:

- replaces the required two-pupil HS entry with a single-pupil option;
- changes the 122 cm × 122 cm × 100 cm limit or assigns its axes;
- supplies venue internet, guarantees a power outlet, or sets cloud/API access rules;
- explicitly permits or prohibits LLM APIs, downloaded models, third-party libraries or commercial microcontroller components for this prepared event;
- extends the Electronics/IoT/Robotics on-the-spot construction and preassembled-system restrictions to prepared Working Model.

This is the boundary of the recovered evidence, not proof that no local or later instruction exists. Karen's saved school registration, a later applicable circular and an item-specific organiser notice are the records needed to settle those questions. Neither silence nor a model's hardware architecture establishes permission, prohibition or reclassification.

For knowledge ingestion, retain at least **document date, portal edition, fair, section, item code, competition level, authority, source page, and unresolved conflict status**. Versioned facts such as quiz group size and time limits should be retrieved with their date and scope. The LP Social Science mismatch and the older UP progression contradiction should remain visible conflicts rather than being flattened into confident answers.
