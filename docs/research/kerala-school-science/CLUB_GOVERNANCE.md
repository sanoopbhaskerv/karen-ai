# Subject-club governance and election directions

Checked **1 November 2026**. Two publicly mirrored DGE circulars, dated **4 July 2024** and **20 May 2026**, give dated institutional context for the Science, Mathematics, Social Science and Work Experience clubs. The [source register](club-governance-sources.json) retains each visible departmental reference, signature block, page count and downloaded-file hash. Original government-hosted copies were not recovered in the bounded lookup; the mirror hosting and visible document content remain distinct. No cryptographic signature validation was performed.

## Terms and the election sequence

Both circulars explicitly state **one year for school clubs** and **two years for subdistrict and revenue-district clubs**. They say the current terms have ended and direct reconstitution. This does not identify the exact earlier election/expiry dates, establish a state-club term, or supply IT/Little Kites rules.

| Directed step | 4 July 2024 circular | 20 May 2026 circular |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AEO convenes the general body of the new school-club office-bearers and elects subdistrict office-bearers | By 10 July 2024 | By 15 June 2026 |
| DDE convenes the new subdistrict office-bearers and elects district office-bearers; working committees constituted under the manual | By 12 July 2024 | By 22 June 2026 |
| Elected secretaries’ particulars forwarded to DGE | By 15 July 2024; AEO/DDE direction | By 26 June 2026; DDE direction |

The June 15 direction is **not a stated school-election deadline**: it concerns electing subdistrict office-bearers from the new school-level office-bearers’ general body. Neither circular proves the directed meetings/elections occurred, provides the successful office-bearers, or imposes a general all-teachers attendance mandate. The references to club roles are not appointments to a particular festival’s organising committee.
