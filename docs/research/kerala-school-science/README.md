# Kerala school science exhibitions research

Initial research checked on 31 October 2026, with archive follow-up on 1 November 2026. This collection covers Kerala School Sasthrolsavam, its Science and IT fairs, earlier regional school fairs, historical state editions, participation rules, official results, special-school provision, the separate Technical High School fair, and related regional and national science programmes. It is intended as evidence for Karen's future knowledge collection and as a reference for the student's exhibition preparation.

The collection remains open. Early history, some annual records, original project reports, and the exact registration category for Karen still need stronger evidence. An accessible results website is not a complete historical archive.

## How to read conflicting records

An annual circular may qualify the general manual. A later result revision may differ from a dated press report. Keep both source date and event year with the claim. The history notes record disagreements rather than selecting a score without an explanation. Likewise, a school's local notice can identify its event and arrangements, but cannot establish an amendment to state rules unless it cites the responsible authority.

For a rule affecting participation, prefer the current signed departmental or KITE circular and the applicable manual chapter. For an annual result, preserve the item, level, edition and publication status. Broad promotional descriptions and undated About pages cannot settle exact item counts, historical rankings, or today's eligibility.

## Collection boundaries

This research adds reference material only. It does not train a model, connect a retrieval service, change Karen's saved identity or runtime memory, or establish that Karen has qualified for a particular event. It also does not claim every historical exhibit has a publicly accessible abstract or report. Source links and original factual summaries are retained so the collection can grow without blending editions or programmes.
