Checked **1 November 2026**. Three DGE documents add dated appeal and Work Experience provisions from **2023–24 and 2025–26**. All six PDF pages were read visually and independently checked. ## Appeals: 27 October 2025

The [two-page appeal direction](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1NYhLTwIOONiliTwUa3PdWof-2TuzxdD8/view), **Y(1)/16000/2025/DGE; DGE/16000/2025-Y1; I/1497607/2025**, addresses DDEs and AEOs for the **2025–26** fair. It refers to the revised manual and a Kerala High Court division-bench judgment, **W.A.1178/2017, 22 June 2017**. This supplement has not independently reviewed that judgment and does not turn the circular into general legal advice.

The circular directs that only two qualifying contestants advance from one level/item. If an appeal would admit a third, an explicit order must also assess the already-qualified performances and exclude one, retaining two advancing contestants. Appeal authorities must follow chapter four of the revised manual. This is a procedural direction for that edition, without a named successful appeal or an explanation for historical championship changes.

This advancement capacity must be kept separate from an exhibit's required group size. It does not reduce Science Working Model to a one-pupil entry. For the current item, use [RULES.md](RULES.md).

The body schedules the state festival for **7–10 November 2025 in Palakkad**; it does not prove completion. Reference 1 visibly combines **23 August 2025** with **G.O.(Ms)146/2015**. Preserve that printed year mismatch beside the separately recovered **146/2025** order in [RULE_CHANGES.md](RULE_CHANGES.md). PDF 2 has the visible Santhosh C A signature block, **27 October 2025, 12:57:33**, Additional Director of General Education (Academic). Source `historical-operations-appeal-2025`.

## Work Experience: 29 October 2025

The [two-page Work Experience circular](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1NdUG2iIH0-0quz37w9lg4jJMRRbKgls_/view), **WE2/4071835/2025/DGE**, schedules the state Work Experience fair for **7–10 November 2025 at BEM HSS, Palakkad**. It allocates **8 November to special-school pupils, 9 November to HS and 10 November to HSS/VHSS**. These are historical plans, rather than proof of attendance or Karen's reporting instructions.

| Named Work Experience item | Dated clarification | PDF page |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Products using areca leaf sheath (പാള) | A whole piece may be prepared in one colour; partial colouring, multiple colours on one piece and coloured designs are prohibited. | 1 |
| Innovative Working Model | Present at most three socially useful new ideas and use prototype forms to explain their practical operation. | 1 |

**Innovative Working Model here belongs to Work Experience.** Its name does not make it Science Working Model. The distinction is also visible in the separately versioned [catalogues](ACCESS.md#item-catalogue-snapshots).

The Innovative Working Model clause allows a needed **mobile/laptop explanation only with both the judge's and invigilator's permission**. The closing Work Experience clause prohibits contestants from possessing or using **mobiles/smartwatches during competition**. Retain the specific permission and general restriction together. Neither establishes a blanket laptop ban, a Science Working Model network rule, cloud-API permission or a 2026 local exception.

## Work Experience: 9 October 2023

The [two-page 2023 circular](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VbOV6_GZSdIauHFTjKbhbFlDj7VSYTxJ/view), **WE3/1128176/2023/DGE**, schedules the general and special-school state Work Experience fairs for **30 November–2 December 2023 in Thiruvananthapuram**. It introduces a revised on-the-spot/exhibition sequence for that year.

| Historical programme phase | Printed clock interval, normalised to 24-hour time |
| --- | --- |
| On-the-spot construction | 09:30–12:30 |
| Final-phase evaluation | 12:30–13:30 |
| Lunch and exhibition preparation | 13:30–14:30 |
| Exhibition competition and evaluation | 14:30–16:00 |
| Public viewing | From 15:30 |

The circular explicitly provides **no exhibition competition at subdistrict level**, while district/state have one. Read its compulsory exhibition-participation direction with that qualification. Exhibition includes all products from the on-the-spot item; the individual and exhibition marks jointly determine the overall championship. Display/evaluation uses the same competition location, and contestants must remain until exhibition evaluation finishes.

For the state sequence, the first day is special-school, the second HS and the third HSS/VHSS. Public viewing overlaps the stated exhibition/evaluation window; no different end time is invented. These directions do not supply Science Working Model's presentation duration or a current 2026 timetable.

PDF 2 has a handwritten signature and the printed Malayalam name normalised as **M K Shinemon**, Additional Director of General Education (Academic). Employee identifiers and contact fields were not copied. Source `historical-operations-workexperience-2023`.

## A specific district funding request rejected in 2024

The **17 July 2024** state decentralised-planning coordination proceedings record Kasaragod District Panchayat's request to use **₹1 lakh each** from own funds for district school **sports, science and arts fairs: ₹3 lakh total**. Item **4.11** cites letters dated **26 March 2024** and **7 October 2023**, mentions the previous year's October–December fairs collectively and seeks payment after the financial year. These are not exact dates assigned to each fair. The meeting date is printed in the title; a separate issue date is not established. [LSGD proceedings, PDF 1, 8–9 / printed 7–8](https://lsgkerala.gov.in/system/files/2024-07/CCMinutes17.07.2024.pdf#page=8)

The decision explicitly **rejects the request**, without stating a reason here. Requested sums are not grants or disbursements, and this particular decision does not establish a universal funding ban. The fourteen-page file received native-text screening; PDF **1, 8 and 9** supplied visual review of the title, references and complete relevant item. Adjacent permissions and unrelated personal/medical cases are excluded. Source `historical-operations-kasaragod-funding-2024`.
