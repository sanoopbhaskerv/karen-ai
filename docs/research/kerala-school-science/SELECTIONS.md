# Kerala entries in NCERT organiser selection records

Checked on **1 November 2026**. The collection contains **14 selected-exhibit records** and **20 separate participant-form records** for Kerala-located schools in the 2023–2025 national exhibitions. The [versioned index](national-selection-index.json) preserves each source's wording, credits, heading and PDF page. Personal contact fields and photographs are omitted.

An organiser's selected-exhibit list establishes selection for national participation. It does not establish attendance, a prize, which child wrote particular code, or independently tested performance. A participant form supplies another published record of submitted details; its achievements field is not treated here as a certified award list. Kerala-located KVS/NVS schools and entries listed under the Kerala state heading are recorded separately.

## Still needed

Kerala DGE/SCERT nomination proceedings, SISF delegation lists, accepted registration records and final attendance statements would establish more than these national selection snapshots. Signed award records are needed for prize claims; student logs and code history are needed for individual construction or coding contributions. The indexed forms' self-reported awards have not been promoted to verified results.

The eight selected-list/form PDFs remain outside the repository. Their hashes, page counts, access dates, version limits and verification notes are in [pathways-sources.json](pathways-sources.json), source IDs **pathways-07, pathways-30 through pathways-35, and pathways-38**. No participant contact information or photographs are included in the derived collection.
