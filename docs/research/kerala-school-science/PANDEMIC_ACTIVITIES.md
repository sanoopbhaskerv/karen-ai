# Pandemic period: competition decisions and separate online programmes

Checked **1 November 2026**. These five documents add primary governmental context and mirrored departmental instructions for 2020–21 and 2021–22. Their scopes differ: a grace-mark decision, a report that departmental competitions could not be held, and directions for separate online science programmes. They do **not** supply standalone year-specific Kerala State Sasthrolsavam cancellation/rescheduling orders. Keep the separate NCERT national-exhibition chronology in [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md) distinct.

## Government decisions and the reported interruption

The [General Education Department letter of 29 June 2021](https://drive.google.com/file/d/18XIC4gP7RZg71O7dDZwbyqgGZB3iptM2/view?usp=sharing), reference G/1055/2021, communicates the decision not to award grace marks in the **2020–21 public examinations**. The recovered one-page copy is a public Drive mirror. It does not itself explain which fairs were cancelled or give an event cancellation date. Its printed date and visible approval block are 29 June; the discovery index labels the link 28 June. Source `pandemic-activity-002`.

The original-hosted [G.O.(Rt)3563/2022/GEDN of 14 June 2022](https://education.kerala.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/GO-3563-Grace-mark-14.06.2022.pdf#page=1), PDF 1, records the DGE's report that restrictions prevented the department's arts, sports and science competitions in **2021–22**. It then orders no grace marks in that year's SSLC/Plus Two examinations. It also recalls the preceding year's no-grace decision. This strengthens the primary evidence for an interruption in the departmental competition context, without identifying a particular Sasthrolsavam cancellation order or proving that every school, club or online science activity stopped. The visible Section Officer block is on PDF 2; cryptographic validity was not tested. Source `pandemic-activity-001`.

These are historical examination decisions. Neither sets Karen's 2026 grace-mark entitlement.

## Shasthrarangam: expressly directed online activity

A [DGE proceedings copy dated 15 February 2021](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1eR-V07NW2Sj4unim983NydFjctFe4KOy/view?usp=sharing), V.R.9677/20, extends **Vidyarangam and Shasthrarangam** to Higher Secondary and Vocational Higher Secondary from **2021–22**. This one-page public mirror concerns those programmes, rather than a statewide fair's inauguration or resumption. Source `pandemic-activity-004`.

The [four-page circular V.R.248106/2021, dated 12 July 2021](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1IaM82NemYU8hIfYzuoVrCKXgz0N03FDF/view?usp=sharing), explicitly directs **online/digital Shasthrarangam activities because of Covid**, with school, subdistrict, district and state stages. Its named activities include project presentations, experiments using materials at home, local history, scientific writing, scientist biographies, science-book reviews and mathematical concept presentations. It assigns institutional support and selection duties, including online access support. These are dated directions, not proof that every stage occurred. This is a mirrored copy; the index labels it 10 July, while the document says 12 July. Source `pandemic-activity-003`.

Do not harmonise its conflicting timing passages:

| Passage | Literal school / subdistrict / district schedule |
| --- | --- |
| PDF 2, activities other than projects | 30 August / 20 September / 30 September |
| PDF 2, projects | 25 September / 10 October / 25 October |
| PDF 3, level descriptions without an explicit item heading | Complete on 25 August / complete on 15 September / complete before 30 September |

PDF 2 separately directs school-unit inaugurations online by 25 July and forwarding selected district work to the state within five days. The conflicting passages require a later clarification or amendment before selecting one precise competition timetable. These historical online directions neither replace Science Working Model rules nor establish permission for Karen's present cloud services.

## NCSC: another programme, with a printed calendar anomaly

The original-hosted [DGE circular QIP(1)/210114/2020/DGE of 9 December 2020](https://education.kerala.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/ncsc_9.12.2020.pdf), PDF 1–2, concerns the **28th National Children's Science Congress**, with KSCSTE as the Kerala nodal agency. PDF 2 expressly says all its programmes will be online because of the pandemic. It requests institutional/teacher assistance and describes planned district and state stages. This is NCSC, rather than Kerala Sasthrolsavam or NCERT's national exhibition. Source `pandemic-activity-005`.

Its PDF 2 timetable prints a teacher workshop in **December 2020**, project preparation as **December–January 2020**, district Congress in the **first week of January 2020**, and state Congress in the **second and third weeks of January 2020**. Those January-year labels conflict with the December 2020 issue/planning sequence. They remain as printed; **January 2021 has not been silently substituted**, and this notice is not evidence that the scheduled events actually happened.
