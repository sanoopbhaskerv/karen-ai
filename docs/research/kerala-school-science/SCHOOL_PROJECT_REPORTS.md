No original build log or verified exhibition-code repository was recovered in this bounded pass.

These are source-specific publication records in [school-project-sources.json](school-project-sources.json), separate from the official write-ups in [MODEL_REPORTS.md](MODEL_REPORTS.md). ## Canal microplastics: a later related publication

[school-project-004](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43832-026-00468-2), published 2 October 2026, has a strong candidate correspondence to teacher model `sisf-2026-kerala-teacher-p197` (compendium PDF 240–241 / printed 197–198). The three sampling sites, extraction/identification sequence and several unusual reported percentage ranges match; the register records the exact basis. Article PDF 4–5, 8 and 10 are the relevant method/result locators.

The compendium credits **Jubymol, George,** at **ST. Anne’s GHSS Changanacherry**. The article credits **Jubymol George, Simimole Sebastian and Reema Rose Mani**, all at **Alphonsa College, Pala** (PDF 1). Received/accepted/publication dates appear on PDF 12. These affiliation and credit differences remain unresolved; no personal-identity or contribution equivalence is asserted. The article abstract prints 0.12–0.5 µm, while the body uses 0.1–0.5 mm (PDF 1, 5 and 8). Preserve this discrepancy. The reported percentage ranges have unclear denominators; shared numbers are correspondence evidence, not a validated composition partition. The compendium also lists KOH among materials while its procedure describes peroxide; the article describes peroxide.

## Bounded search and remaining gaps

All 31 compendium entries received at least one pupil/teacher plus title/topic query. The driving simulator, Medibot, SAMARTH and fog entries received additional name/school/title variants, public repository searches, and Malayalam searches. Public profile/repository candidates and sampled video interfaces supplied no explicit project-report/code bridge. Unrelated name matches, admission/exam lists, generic same-topic papers and social reposts were excluded.

For those four priority exhibits, no verified original report, log or code repository was recovered. This is a search outcome, not evidence that none exists. The four publication PDFs, request/hash ledger, query ledger and page renders remain outside the repository; only bibliographic facts, concise method notes, limitations and candidate references are retained here. No source media or personal contact details are copied into this dataset.
