## Separate history and participation route

The manual’s preface attributes the beginning of this **Technical High School** Science & Technology Fair and the first SITTTR manual to **2017**. Its discussion of technical schools opening in the 1960s concerns the institutions, not the fair’s first edition. These statements do not date the beginning of general-school Sasthrolsavam. (`technical-school-001`, PDF 2 / printed i.)

The manual also reproduces **GO(Rt) No.1789/2017/HEDN, 7 October 2017**, sanctioning use of **20% of the Development Fund** for the technical-school fair, subject to the cited GO(Ms) No.516/2012/H.EDN conditions and SITTTR rules. The lower forwarding note is separately dated **17 October 2017**. The underlying 2012 order has not been reviewed in this packet. (`technical-school-001`, PDF 47 / printed 43.)

The manual specifies:

- **Eligibility:** classes **8–10** in technical high schools under Technical Education and IHRD; three streams—Science & Technology, IT and Mathematics. (PDF 5 / printed 1.)
- **Stages:** school and state. A school first-place contestant/model with **A grade** advances. If no contestant earns first place with A grade, that item is not sent to state; a sole contestant must still meet A-grade performance. (PDF 7 / printed 3; PDF 11 / printed 7; Working Model clause at PDF 32 / printed 28.)
- **Individual limit:** one pupil may enter one item in one stream. A school may send at most **16 pupils** to the state fair; this is a pupil limit, not 16 events. (PDF 7 / printed 3; PDF 13 / printed 9.)
- **Exhibition:** competitors in the onsite/model items also participate in the school exhibition competition; separate new contestants are not allowed for that exhibition. (PDF 7 / printed 3; entry-form note at PDF 48 / printed 44.)

These are the technical manual’s provisions. Its grace-mark paragraph expressly defers to the relevant year’s government orders; its historical wording is not substituted for current general-school grace-mark guidance. (PDF 30 / printed 26.)

## Working Model conditions and rubric

The technical-school Working Model must relate to technical specialisation trades/science and social usefulness. **Two pupils** present the model; one model finishing first with A grade at school may advance to state. A brief exhibit description is required during the competition, and photos may accompany it. (`technical-school-001`, PDF 7 / printed 3; PDF 32 / printed 28.)

The Science & Technology general instructions allocate **two hours** to Working/Still Model competitions and **three hours** to onsite competitions. Working/Still Models must fit within **2.0 m² floor area** and **2.0 m maximum height**. The area is not a 2 m × 2 m footprint. This timing clause alone is not evidence that every prebuilt Working Model must be constructed onsite. (PDF 31 / printed 27.)

The detailed Working/Still Model rubric is:

| Criterion in the detailed rubric | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Scientific Thought/Principle | 25% |
| Creativity, Innovative and Originality | 25% |
| Technical Skill and Workmanship | 30% |
| Utility, Durability and Economic Low Cost | 10% |
| Personal Interview/Presentation | 10% |

Source: `technical-school-001`, §5.5.1, PDF 36 / printed 32. The Working Model evaluation form at PDF 54 / printed 50 agrees on **25/25/30/10/10**, but labels the last two criteria **Social usefulness of the product** and **Viva voce**. Retain the labels of the particular source page when transcribing.

## The seven 2025 amendments

GO(Rt) No.1292/2025/HEDN cites the 2024 approval and the DTE letter **DTETVM/500/2025-C1 (ACA), 26 September 2025**. Its annexure contains these seven Science & Technology Fair amendment rows. The order date is **29 September 2025**; the annexure’s eOffice export line is separately **28 October 2026**. (`technical-school-002`, PDF 1–3.)

| Target clause | Change approved in annexure PDF 3 |
| --- | --- |
| §2.1, school level, second point | Replaces the paragraph’s specified-theme wording with conduct subject to SITTTR conditions and instructions. The base manual’s separate general theme clause remains printed at PDF 5 / printed 1; this amendment is not proof that every form of theme direction was abolished. |
| §5.4.2, Electrical Wiring | Adds strict compliance with safety directions when using modern electrical tools, alongside permission for modern tools. |
| §5.4.3, Sheet Metal Work | Replaces 22/24 SWG metal sheet with **24/26 SWG G.I. sheet**. |
| §5.4.4, Carpentry | Allows planed timber to be brought; cutting, marking, drilling and the remaining specified operations are to be done at the venue. |
| §6.2.2, Animation | Adds **OpenToonz** alongside the already allowed application and directs saving the final export in **MP4/AVI** format. |
| §7.1, Mathematics general conditions | Specifies that the write-up of the idea presented onsite, up to **three pages**, must itself be prepared at the venue and handed to the invigilator. |
| §7.2.1, Applied Construction | Adds application of engineering alongside mathematical concepts in the construction, retaining the chart-size context. |

The annexure does not amend the Working Model pupil count, selection rule, dimensions, duration or rubric. Its electrical/woodworking and Mathematics directions retain their named-item scope.

## The planned 2025 Krishnapuram fair and local-body funding

The DTE-hosted programme advertises the **seventh** All Kerala Technical High School Science & Technology Fair at **Government Technical High School, Krishnapuram**, on **17–19 October 2025**. It schedules inauguration at **15:30 on 17 October**, competitions from **08:30 on 18 October**, Still Model/exhibition competitions on **19 October**, and the closing meeting at **15:00 on 19 October**. Its introductory account anticipates representation from **39 technical high schools and nine IHRD technical higher secondary schools**, and exhibition arrangements for 48 schools. These are programme statements, not independently verified attendance totals or completed activities. (`technical-school-003`, PDF 1–2 and 4.)

The earlier LSGD order’s recital instead gives **18–19 October 2025** in Alappuzha district. Preserve that date scope alongside the programme’s 17–19 dates; this packet does not establish why the descriptions differ. (`technical-school-004`, PDF 1.)

The order permits the following spending by the specified **Alappuzha-district** local bodies:

| Local body described in the order | Authorised amount per body |
| --- | ---: |
| Grama panchayat | ₹8,000 |
| Municipality | ₹10,000 |
| Host municipality | ₹25,000 |
| District panchayat | ₹25,000 |

The expenditure is from each body’s **own fund**, **subject to its governing body’s decision**. This is permission for the named expenditure, not proof of a mandatory collection, an approved individual body resolution, disbursement or final expenditure. The host-municipality amount is retained separately; no additional cumulative ₹10,000 is inferred. (`technical-school-004`, PDF 1; distribution/signature page at PDF 2.)
