# Early administration: contemporary science-education context

Reviewed 1 November 2026. This supplement adds contemporary NCERT journal evidence found while seeking Kerala Education Department administration reports for 1965–66 and 1966–67. It does **not** recover those reports or establish the statewide fair's inaugural year. Keep the regional fairs, statewide series and national exhibition distinct, as in [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md).

## Kerala teachers at a December 1965 workshop

*School Science*, Vol. IV No. 4, December 1965, reports a science-club sponsors' workshop at Madras Christian College High School, Madras, on **19–28 December 1965**. The participants came from Mysore, Kerala and Madras; **21 teachers is the combined total**, with no Kerala subtotal or names. Training included practical craft skills and school investigation projects. More than 100 project outlines were developed, with subsequent school trials described as future work. This records Kerala participation in a workshop outside Kerala, rather than a Kerala fair or an inaugural event. The issue month does not establish its exact distribution date. [Archived NCERT scan](https://archive.org/download/dli.ernet.231487/231487-School%20Science%20Vol-04.pdf#page=361): PDF 361, printed 329; the contents on PDF 293 identify that page number. Source `early-administration-001`.

## Remaining report gap and provenance

Both sources are NCERT publications recovered through Internet Archive copies attributed to the Digital Library of India, C-DAC Noida scanning centre and NCERT source library. The original scans, rather than catalogue dates alone, establish the issue dates and cited wording. Their checksums, sizes, page counts and review scopes are in [early-administration-sources.json](early-administration-sources.json).

This bounded pass also checked public archive and State Central Library catalogues, the formats exposed for the known partial 1965–66 general administration scan, and later economic/statistical reports. The missing Education Department reports and the general report's Education section remain unrecovered here. One misleading search hit, [Ecostat file 1080](https://www.ecostat.kerala.gov.in/storage/publications/1080.pdf), actually has a **Bureau of Economics and Statistics, 1967–68** cover and a **1969** copyright page; it is not the sought Education report. Catalogue-only holdings, failed downloads and OCR nonmatches are not evidence that a fair did not take place. Detailed acquisition and search records remain outside the repository.
