## KSSP review of the 2019 Science Fair

In *Sasthrakeralam*, volume 51, issue 8 (**January 2020**), science teacher **V. V. Venugopalan** reviews the Science Fair within the state Sasthrolsavam. His affiliation is Govt Higher Secondary School, Erumappetty, Thrissur. The [complete article](https://edu.kssp.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Sasthrakeralam-January-2020-web.pdf#page=41), PDF/printed **41–42**, identifies **3–5 November 2019**, **T. M. V. Higher Secondary School, Perumpilavu, Thrissur**, and reports more than 500 pupils from fourteen districts. It says all non-appeal pupil entrants outside Science Quiz received A grades. These remain the author's statements, rather than independently audited attendance or grade totals.

The author praises pupils' explanations and the organisation. He also describes a concentration of improvised experiments in physics, especially optics and electromagnetism; some teaching aids made unnecessarily complicated to win prizes; and pupils leaving after judging, limiting public viewing and exchange between exhibitors. These are attributed observations and recommendations from this edition.

The two-page article supplies no sampling protocol, participant-level data, questionnaire, comparison group or measured learning outcomes. Its claims do not establish prevalence across Kerala, causal educational effects or present participation requirements. The author's teaching affiliation does not establish that he was a judge or an organiser. No pupil identity or personal contact details are retained here.

The issue's publication details on PDF 3 and both article pages were read visually; the full received file has **52 PDF pages**. The institutional publisher is Kerala Sasthra Sahithya Parishath; this publication is not a government instruction. For the broader dated chronology, see [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md).
