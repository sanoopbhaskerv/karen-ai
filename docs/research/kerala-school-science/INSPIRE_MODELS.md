# INSPIRE-MANAK project compendia and Kerala entries

Checked **1 November 2026**. INSPIRE's National Level Exhibition and Project Competition (NLEPC) is a separate programme from Kerala's Sasthrolsavam. Its organiser compendia add short project descriptions and source-specific credits; they do not supply a Kerala Sasthrolsavam rank, an original school logbook or independent scientific validation. The [structured register](inspire-nlepc-kerala-projects.json) keeps programme, edition, dates, page references and evidence limits together.

## Public publication index

The official [compendium index](https://www.inspireawards-dst.gov.in/UserP/nlepc-compendium.aspx), source `pathways-54`, advertises **18 volume links** across the first through eleventh NLEPC labels. This is a link inventory, not 18 distinct editions or 18 completely reviewed books. Both eleventh-edition labels say “Vol II” despite different `-1`/`-2` filenames; the received labels remain literal in the dataset. Downloaded bodies must establish their own volume and date context.

The nearby generic JNV entry supplies no locality and remains geographically unassigned.

Both full newer files were processed with machine OCR for discovery because their original text layers are inadequate. All five Kerala entries, the full KVS/NVS sections and target front matter/boundaries were visually reviewed. OCR supplied no further location candidate in the bounded screen; that is not proof of absence or a full human reading of every page. Some render sizes clipped metadata, so complete adopted fields were checked against legible alternate renders. Original PDFs were not altered.

The register now contains **16 descriptions** across the seventh and eleventh editions. The four books total **827 PDF pages**; these are file inventories, not a claim that every national project was visually audited. Short mechanisms and proposed benefits remain distinct from original code, measured field performance, safety validation and individual work allocation. No final prize rank is inferred from compendium order or a nonmatch in a separate winner list.
