# INSPIRE-MANAK project compendia and Kerala entries

Checked **1 November 2026**. INSPIRE's National Level Exhibition and Project Competition (NLEPC) is a separate programme from Kerala's Sasthrolsavam. Its organiser compendia add short project descriptions and source-specific credits; they do not supply a Kerala Sasthrolsavam rank, an original school logbook or independent scientific validation. The [structured register](inspire-nlepc-kerala-projects.json) keeps programme, edition, dates, page references and evidence limits together.

## Public publication index

The official [compendium index](https://www.inspireawards-dst.gov.in/UserP/nlepc-compendium.aspx), source `pathways-54`, advertises **18 volume links** across the first through eleventh NLEPC labels. This is a link inventory, not 18 distinct editions or 18 completely reviewed books. Both eleventh-edition labels say “Vol II” despite different `-1`/`-2` filenames; the received labels remain literal in the dataset. Downloaded bodies must establish their own volume and date context.
